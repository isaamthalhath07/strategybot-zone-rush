# Independent result review

The frozen teacher's mean change was **-0.03125 pairwise points** across the
sixteen preregistered positions. Its descriptive 95% bootstrap interval was
**[-0.21875, 0.15625]**. It changed eight root recommendations: two positions
improved, three worsened and eleven had the same final pairwise result. This
sample provides no positive evidence that the teacher improves decisions;
the interval also does not establish a reliable harmful effect.

The evaluator's best legal one-step alternative improved on baseline by a
mean **0.1875 points**, with descriptive interval **[0.0625, 0.375]**. This is
an outcome-aware diagnostic across the evaluated alternatives. The teacher
did not have those realized outcomes when recommending, and this maximum is
not an executable public policy or a globally optimal strategy.

Independent recomputation found no artifact or arithmetic disagreement. It
bound the prepared registration to the exact published commit
`816a30a101b1280d753b9e8d040b84f37cccbeeb` and checked all twenty-one runtime
source files against both registered hashes and that commit. All seven raw
stage artifacts, accepted snapshot, catalog/manifest and exclusion evidence
were hash-bound. It independently derived all 9,767 eligible public roots
from 2,500 incumbent TRAIN source rows and recovered the exact sixteen
minimum-margin/fixture choices after exclusions. The selected public prefixes
were separately checked through 864 prior turns in both referees, including
public capture/income score accounting, eligibility, margins and input hashes.

All sixteen cases and all ninety-one legal root alternatives are present.
Together with sixteen unchanged baselines, **107 full branch traces** were
independently replayed through both frozen referees: **8,560 physical turns**.
Every recorded action was legal in both engines; full physical states, income,
public score accounting, terminal scores and official pairwise points agreed.
Every unchanged baseline and explicit baseline-action intervention reproduced
the complete original eighty-turn tape. Rivals' actions on the intervention
turn matched baseline, as required by simultaneous decisions.

The complete execution receipts account for **34,240 controller callbacks**,
with eighty calls per seat per branch and finite timing records within the
first/regular deadlines. This reviewer did not independently execute the
controllers again. Actual regeneration is supported by the frozen reviewed
continuation code and measured execution receipts, while this review directly
verified their saved action traces and physical consequences. It does not
turn those receipt counts into an independent second controller execution.

All teacher inputs contain the exact registered public prefixes and bounded
search settings. The sixteen recommendations were reconstructed from saved
root statistics. Arithmetic checks covered all **1,536 simulated trajectories**
and **9,216 steps**: public observation links, terminal handling, leaf utilities,
node/edge visits and value backups, root means and recommendation tie breaks.
This result review did not repeat latent posterior inference, sampling or
hypothetical physics. Its evidence for the real intervention effects comes
from the independently replayed actual branch traces, rather than the
teacher's predicted values.

Every per-case teacher delta and hindsight diagnostic was recomputed from
the replayed terminal outcomes. An independent implementation reproduced all
20,000 shared within-field bootstrap draws, their draw digest, field means,
quantiles and the complete reported statistics exactly. The raw ZIP's 316
JSON/sidecar members were compared byte-for-byte with the local run; every
seal and the archive manifest matched. There is no failure artifact or omitted
registered case in this completed run.

These are deliberately selected, already consumed TRAIN positions with four
maps per rival field. The intervention synchronizes accepted-action memory
while retaining the focal planner's original target/bait updates; it is a
one-step hybrid diagnostic. Its bootstrap interval describes this small
selected-position sample and the one frozen teacher seed per case. It does
not cover fresh-map policy strength, opponent-model misspecification, teacher
seed variability, selection uncertainty or later tuning. Offline teacher time
does not establish a 200 ms deployed actor. No source, plan, search cap or
policy was changed after observing outcomes during this review.

This experiment grants **zero new primary games**, **zero completed per-game
BMCTS audits**, and **no promotion or deployment qualification**. The separate
predictor's forecast-loss improvement remains distinct from playing strength.
