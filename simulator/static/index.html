Every move matters.

Outscore three opponents over 80 turns. All four players choose before the turn resolves.

  1. Move one square north, east, south or west. A square occupied at the start of a turn blocks entry.
  2. Stay to claim. A neutral square becomes yours after one stay. Its number is income paid every turn.
  3. Stay to upgrade. Three consecutive stays on your own square double its income. Moving resets your progress.
  4. Raid rival land. One stay captures a basic square; an upgraded square takes three. A raid earns a bonus of 8 × base value × old level. Homes cannot be captured.
  5. Shield your land. After movement, freeze eligible rivals on any of your land for two turns, including arrivals and moves between your cells. Rivals who leave your land escape. Catch nobody eligible and you freeze instead. Your shield then rests for four turns.

A square’s owner wins a contested entry. Otherwise the rotating priority token decides. Your home can be upgraded.
