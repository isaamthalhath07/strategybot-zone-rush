# Analysis after complete TRAIN admission

This is a prospective analysis guide, not permission to inspect a partial
generation. No TRAIN generation0/1 outcome, live study result, DEV/FINAL score,
key, controller or GPU operation was consumed to write it. Use only a whole
generation admitted by the root authority after complete source, transcript,
timing, independent actor regeneration and two-referee checks. Preserve every
failure and partial result outside selection. An arithmetic ranking is not an
admission certificate.

## What the final promotion rule does

The frozen rule in [evaluation.py](../deadline_campaign/evaluation.py) requires
an overall two-sided95% percentile-bootstrap lower limit above0 and all four
one-sided Bonferroni field lower limits above−0.05 pairwise points. Otherwise
it retains the fixed qualified anchor. It can reject useful but imprecisely
estimated improvements; that conservative fallback is consistent with the
deadline. It also deliberately permits a true field decline smaller than0.05
when the other conditions pass. The rule optimizes the equal-field mean with
that preregistered robustness margin, not every matchup or unknown opponent.

This is an intersection-union decision: the unfavorable composite null is
`overall_delta<=0 OR some_field_delta<=−0.05`. With exact valid one-sided tests,
requiring every test to pass does not create a multiple-testing false-promotion
problem merely because there are five criteria. For any parameter in that
null, rejection requires rejection of at least one true component null. The
actual finite-map percentile bootstrap is approximate, however, so it supplies
no exact type-I bound. Do not turn the separate intervals into a guaranteed
joint five-claim confidence statement. The field Bonferroni limits are already
more stringent than an unadjusted field criterion.

Freeze one challenger and one anchor before the untouched final cohort. Their
selection from TRAIN/DEV is allowed; the fresh final comparison does not need
to retest every historical candidate, provided it is genuinely unused, the
final contrast is fixed once, and failure never triggers another final draw.
The final anchor must be fixed from complete independently admitted evidence
and be qualified under the delivery runtime. If DEV selects that anchor, a
predeclared no-promotion fallback avoids inventing a second challenger after
viewing final results. Neither final outcomes nor its confidence limits may
choose coefficients, a goal hazard, a field mixture or new stopping times.

## Exact quantities for generation0 and generation1

For map m, arm a and field f, let

`C[a,m,f] = Σ_(16 seat/token cells) 2·U_focal`,

an integer0..96. For two arms a,b, define per-map field contrast
`D[m,f]=(C[a,m,f]−C[b,m,f])/32`; per-map overall contrast is the mean of its four
fields. The reported overall delta is the mean over the16 maps. This matches
the complete64-cell standardized fitness; no game's raw score substitutes for
official pairwise utility. Ranking ties use the frozen worst-field, own score
and SHA order.

Every arm is evaluated on the same maps and seat/token/field grid, but its TRAIN
rivals have fresh **independent** parameter/RNG streams. Call this map-matched
or map-blocked evidence. Do not claim identical-lineup paired effects or replay
a rival future tape after a policy diverges. Different identities alone would
not imply independent streams; the actual registered seed/spec construction is
what establishes that distinction.

Report all eight source hashes, exact structural/numeric family, parent hashes,
operator seeds/attempts, complete counts, means, field means, worst field,
own-score summaries, decision timings and any fault. Then report **every
predeclared contrast**, including zero/negative results. Generation0 now retains
exact learned stage4 offspring_07 (`8d988c...bbb14`) instead of a random e8
child, alongside e8,50d,Blocker,Feint,f5 and the two Blocker/Feint numeric
children. The old standardized stage4 winner50d was already retained; this
additional learned source preserves a distinct evaluated genotype, without a
confirmed-strength claim. Report all28 unordered SHA-lexical arm pairs with
A−B orientation, unadjusted95% and descriptive Bonferroni28 overall intervals.
Useful family contrasts include 50d/e8, Blocker/e8, Feint/e8, f5/original
Blocker, learned8d/e8 and the two specific numeric children/their originals.
For generation1 the new structural contrast is fixed goal/default f5 versus
fixed default f5 in that same generation. State the remaining control/child
contrasts exactly as sealed in its registration. Do not add a favorable
source-selected contrast later and label it primary. A child whose selected
parent is absent from its generation can be compared with the retained original
reference; it cannot be compared causally with its parent's older raw mean.

Resample whole maps, using the same map indices jointly for every arm and field
within a replicate. Use the source-frozen seed, count and quantiles. Report the
registered multiplicity convention for the fixed contrast family. Sixteen map
clusters allow coarse, unstable intervals;1,024 games per arm are not1,024
independent maps. The bootstrap also retains the observed finite rival-stream
realizations; it does not identify their variance separately from map effects.
Do not infer precision from callback count or collapse fields with unequal
weights. Per-field/subset diagnostics beyond the registration are exploratory.

## What can and cannot support a strategy conclusion

The f5/original contrast tests the fixed public-memory correction under common
numeric defaults. A gain is evidence for this correction package on the tested
mixture, not a full rival-cooldown posterior or a universal strategy. The
goal/f5 contrast changes persistent target scores **and** their fixed likelihood/
forgetting calculation. It is a structural package comparison, not a one-factor
isolation of persistence. Numeric child/reference contrasts evaluate those
specific proposals, not numeric optimization in general.

Generation1 elites and numeric parents depend on admitted generation0 fitness.
That adaptivity is expected TRAIN use. Report it and preserve the lineage; a
selected child's apparent gain has selection uncertainty. Generation2 and
later must rank only the immediately previous complete admitted generation,
never compare raw means across different maps, rivals or phases. Fixed
references in every generation permit within-generation comparisons; they do
not erase changing matchup distributions or winner's curse. No retained elite
earns a permanent slot or final-policy status through an unadjusted TRAIN rank.

Useful mechanism summaries, if logged or reconstructed after admission, include
the fraction of changed actions, target persistence, target-score entropy,
invalid-target resets, and whether Shield/progress memory obeyed public
evidence. Do not define a new favorable subset using final outcomes or observed
hindsight headroom. A lower forecast loss is an auxiliary diagnostic; it cannot
override the registered arm layout or become a win label. If forecasts are
compared on saved histories, give both models the **same public prefix and
accepted own actions**, score only the next public observation, and report
exactly how any next-position distribution is normalized. Existing `stepp`
scores discard some goal mass and are not automatically a calibrated
probability distribution.

Keep the fixed goal hazard0.2 and moved factor4 unchanged through this panel.
Use all admitted maps/fields; do not rerun a disappointing cell, replace a
low-headroom map, change the population after partial results, or spend DEV/
FINAL to tune a switch threshold. An inconclusive or negative goal contrast
can legitimately leave f5/original references in later selection. Positive
TRAIN evidence still needs the frozen DEV/final process and source/deadline
qualification. The defensible delivery claim is the strongest policy supported
by this completed bounded protocol, with its uncertainty and limitations;
neither global optimality nor immunity to unseen algorithms follows.
