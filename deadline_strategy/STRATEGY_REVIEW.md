# Strategy work within the deadline

The smallest useful plan is to compare the strong existing source families,
the already published f5 memory correction and prespecified numeric children
in one fully crossed TRAIN panel. Test the fix before adding a larger search
system. The existing intent teacher improved forecast loss in a separate
pilot but did not improve its sixteen causal interventions; a larger search
budget is not a demonstrated strategy gain. No incomplete architecture result,
current study score, DEV/FINAL outcome or new strength game was read or run for
this review.

## What the public evidence establishes

The [architecture preregistration](../confirmation_campaign/ARCHITECTURE_STUDY.md)
records consumed selection evidence from the older 24-map late panel: original
Blocker 2.2148, original Feint 2.1645 and e8 1.9553 pairwise points. That supports
including these source families; it does not establish the ordering under the
new field mixture. The 50d arm is a numeric relative of e8, not an independent
architecture. No result of the ongoing four-arm confirmation is used here.

The [published memory review](../strategy_candidates/README.md) and its pinned
builder describe a reproducible mechanics failure: an unrelated rival Shield
backfire can be mistaken for a landowner's Shield, inventing its cooldown and
suppressing real own raid progress. The f5 correction uses ownership before
work and rejects owner-Shield explanations contradicted by public movement,
known own accepted actions or an eligible stationary focal player escaping a
catch. A blocked accepted move is effective STAY. The published nine scripted
tests and 500-seed memory probe establish mechanics on their tested histories,
with zero strength credit. They do not prove every inferred rival cooldown.

The [prediction pilot](../opponent_prediction/README.md) reports lower mean
public-transition log loss for persistent hypothetical styles than a per-turn
mixture, 2.43887 versus 2.83763, on sixteen consumed TRAIN histories. The
[causal result review](../intent_trials/evidence/causal_001/RESULT_REVIEW.md)
instead reports teacher pairwise delta -0.03125, descriptive interval
[-0.21875, 0.15625], over sixteen positions. Prediction improvement, one-step
hybrid interventions and stronger full-match control are different claims.

## Ranked strategy issues

1. **Correct own memory before trusting adaptive planning.** The f5 correction
   is a justified structural arm, not a strength-certified replacement. Both
   original and f5 retain the same numeric defaults, planner, direct
   `player.act` entry and public-seat RNG initialization. Thus their source
   difference is specifically the published observer correction. Its feedback
   can change later goals and RNG consumption, so their trajectories can
   diverge. Rejection evidence is an admitted fresh negative full-match rank
   contrast, or any legality/deadline/public-memory regression. An interval
   containing zero is inconclusive rather than proof of equivalence.

2. **Use persistent goal evidence before a new expensive search.**
   [Blocker's `_forecast`](../tester_bots/blocker.py) recomputes target scores
   from the current board and five recent displacement likelihood factors.
   Its existing target, response and aggression memories already make it an
   adaptive policy; changing priors alone cannot add an absent plan state.
   A small later structural candidate could retain one public target-mass
   vector per rival, update it once per observation and use it in the existing
   conflict/route/sniping terms. For example, prospectively fix

   `w_t(g) ∝ L_t(g) [(1−η) w_(t−1)(g) + η q_t(g)]`,

   with `η=0.2`, the current board attractiveness as normalized `q_t`, and a
   fixed moved-step factor `L_t(g)=exp(log(4)·[d(previous,g)−d(current,g)])`.
   Set `L=1` for stationary observations and frozen players; blocked intentions
   and simultaneous contests prevent identifying intentional STAY from lack
   of movement. Remove invalid targets and normalize or restart from `q_t`
   under an explicit public rule. These are heuristic normalized goal scores,
   not a Bayesian posterior over actual controllers or goals. The fixed hazard
   can overcommit to a feint or underreact after a goal is completed. Reject
   the feature if public-history prediction is worse or the subsequent fresh
   full-match contrast does not justify its added state and latency. Do not
   fit η, the moved-step factor or a switching threshold on the current panel.

3. **Align denial with own rank boundaries.** Official utility is

   `U_f(s)=Σ_(j≠f) [1(s_f>s_j)+½·1(s_f=s_j)]`.

   For a smoothed finishing-gap model, marginal denial of rival j is
   proportional to its gap density at zero; marginal own score is the sum of
   those densities. Blocker partly uses this logic, but adds leader-specific
   route, parking and Shield premiums. Damaging the highest projected leader
   can sacrifice a closer pairwise boundary and help other rivals. Keep the
   already reviewed leader-bump cap [0,0.2] and report field tradeoffs. A later
   explicit candidate could select the denial rival by boundary sensitivity,
   with a fixed public persistence rule; no claim follows that it dominates
   leader targeting, because the spatial consequences are not isolated score
   transfers. Separately, `_update_alpha` and `_pre` project `80−t` income from
   a pre-turn-t view, while the rules leave `81−t` scoring transitions. That is
   a concrete prospective projection-clock arm; changing this heuristic is
   not a proven improvement and is outside the exact f5 adapter.

4. **Preserve continuation and information boundaries.** A root-only evaluator
   that resumes the exact focal policy is a different package from replacing
   only MCTS fallback rollouts. The frozen teacher's own continuation mismatch,
   rival-model error, short-horizon valuation and finite sampling cannot be
   separated by its realized sixteen-case result. Case13 lost despite full
   terminal coverage, so short horizon alone is insufficient. The completed
   [strategy critique](../intent_trials/evidence/causal_001/STRATEGY_REVIEW.md)
   supports testing the smaller aligned package before adding latent-goal
   search. The CPU reference has no deployed 200 ms or strength qualification.

All candidates must retain simultaneous actions from the same pre-turn view,
start-of-turn occupied-cell blocking, owner/token contests, Shield-before-work,
effective-STAY catches and blocked-move work, frozen work rules, cooldown ticks,
home immunity to capture, upgrade work, pre-capture upgrade bonuses and income
after ownership work. A public projection uses independently reconstructed
scores and public current income. Observer referee counters, actual rival
source/seed/type, future actions and heldout outcomes cannot become features.

For the exact Blocker/Feint/f5/goal templates, normal action-return legality
can also be checked from source: `_decide` returns an offered single action,
membership-gated SHIELD, always-offered STAY, or `_work`/`_move`. `_hop` and
`_move` membership-filter every candidate; `_work` uses the former or STAY,
and Feint delegates movement to the same core. The standalone entry calls
`player.act` directly and exceptions propagate. Their retained internal
illegal-proposal normalization is therefore unreachable on normal return
under the strict legal-mask contract. Current receipts measure accepted
actions, not that internal guard's count; do not describe the source argument
as a measured zero-fallback counter.
