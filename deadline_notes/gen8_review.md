**PB: f5 structure with three numeric changes.** Relative to default f5:

- `_Core.ALPHA:0.34→0.255005`;
- `_Core.CLAIM_THR:0.35→0.416365`;
- `BlockerBot.DENY:0.4→0.436869`.

This uses the public-memory correction and contains no persistent-goal extension (`441596...`). Under fixed `DYN=True`, ALPHA is mainly active in the protection premium; it is not the ordinary dynamic rival-denial weight. In `_prot`, the premium is proportional to `PROT·threat·base·[R(1+ALPHA)/2+8ALPHA]`, so lower ALPHA reduces it at a fixed public context. Higher CLAIM_THR discounts bare neutral claims more under raid threat. Higher DENY raises one leader-focused neutral-cell denial bonus; it is not a master switch for route blocking, parking, sniping or all raids.

A plausible package interpretation is shifting value away from broad protection toward more selective/threat-aware claiming and targeted leader denial. These terms can oppose each other, so "more aggressive" or "more defensive" is inadequate. The report has no action/target/Shield attribution proving which term mattered. Default f5 is absent from this panel, so PB versus original B combines the memory correction and three numeric changes. Neither component has been isolated.

## Adaptation, overfitting and permitted interpretation

GEN008 is an adaptively evolved population. The exact numerical proposals were fixed before its fresh sixteen-map block, but past TRAIN ranks selected their ancestors and surviving templates. Within-generation ranking is allowed by the protocol; it does not erase winner's-curse uncertainty or turn the point maximum into a globally best policy. Multiple generations add source-selection opportunities. A Bonferroni 28 label here addresses this fixed current panel approximately, not all historical hypotheses or a universal algorithm family.

Do not compare the 2.420898 raw mean with earlier generation means as an improvement curve: maps, rival realizations and candidate composition change. The original-B architecture study supports that exact original reference on its own 128-map mixture; this current f5 numeric source cannot inherit its interval or an exact official-generator claim. The custom-map mixture and unknown-rival domain limits still apply. Fresh instances/parameters do not create new opponent algorithms, and lawful public-only state use does not itself establish calibration or strategic optimality.

No goal-template arm appears here, so this result cannot retrospectively rescue the unresolved GEN1 goal extension. Likewise, PB's rank does not prove the f5 correctness fix increased strength by itself. The code-level repair and the noisy outcome comparison are separate facts. Concrete event mechanisms, activation frequency and counterfactual gains remain unmeasured in this review.

The result is promising but inconclusive exploratory TRAIN evidence. Only the registered evolution rule may use the point ranking for its next population; this interpretation cannot alter sources, mutation parameters, controls, field weights, cases, stopping times, DEV/FINAL selection or the separate official-generator check. Do not merge favorable genes, create a hidden field switch, add a posthoc subset winner or rerun a losing case.
